# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Serious art collectors and design-conscious buyers encountering Spencer Grey's work for the first time, or returning to buy a specific piece. They're in a considered mood, not browsing casually. They want to feel something before they commit. The job to be done: move them enough to own the work.

Secondary: fans who follow Spencer's process and want the behind-the-scenes, not just the finished product.

## Product Purpose

An artist portfolio and print store for Spencer Grey. The site exists to make someone feel the work before they buy it. Success is a visitor spending time with a single piece and clicking "add to cart" because they can't not. Not a gallery directory, not a marketplace — a curated encounter with one artist's vision.

## Brand Personality

Raw. Intimate. Cinematic.

Not gallery-white polished. Closer to a studio visit — textures, process, the work in its natural state. The site should feel like you're seeing something before it was cleaned up for public consumption. Emotional pull over aesthetic safety.

Reference: roburico.com — dark, full-bleed, the photography commands the frame, near-zero copy, everything subordinate to the image.

## Anti-references

- Etsy / craft marketplace: no handmade-hobby textures, no DIY warmth, no rustic craft-fair vibes
- Generic artist portfolio template: no plain-white grid with headshot + bio, no cookie-cutter layout
- SaaS or startup: no feature bullets, no metric callouts, no tech-startup visual language

## Design Principles

1. **The work commands the frame.** Imagery is sovereign — never decorative, never thumbnailed into oblivion. Full-bleed or nothing.
2. **Intimacy over polish.** Rough proximity to process beats sterile gallery presentation. Let the seams show.
3. **Black is the gallery wall.** Dark surfaces make the color in Spencer's work pop. The cream background works against the art, not for it.
4. **Earn the collector's patience.** Slow reveals, deliberate pacing. This is not a site you rush through.
5. **Copy earns every word.** Minimal text. What's there lands with weight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Dark bg requires strict contrast checks on all text — especially muted/secondary copy which often fails on near-black surfaces. Reduced motion alternative for all entrance animations. Alt text written in the brand voice ("Gold acrylic on canvas, 24×36", not "artwork image").
